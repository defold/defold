What servers.com actually offers

Now operating as "Servers.com by Nexcess" under CloudOne Digital. Product line: Scalable & Enterprise Bare Metal, Cloud Servers (OpenStack/KVM), regional Load Balancing, Swift-based Cloud Storage, Managed Kubernetes (bundled free with bare metal), Private Racks. No CDN, no DNS product, no backup-as-a-service, no global anycast HTTPS LB with managed SSL — and no Germany or Belgium datacenter (closest EU site is Amsterdam, ~170km from GCP's europe-west1).
